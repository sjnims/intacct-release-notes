# 2024 R2 Executive Summary

**BLUF:** 2024 R2 is a broad release headlined by three GA milestones — the new Fixed Assets Management application (with automatic asset creation from vendor invoices), embedded Sage Intelligent Time, and PwC Control Insights — plus the Open Beta of the new REST API. AP Automation's AI predictions for GL account and dimensions are now extended to all AP Automation customers, and there is meaningful new regional tax content for France, Canada, the UK, Australia, and South Africa. The main planning items are the retirement of Gantt charts in Projects, the new 2-user cap on designated support contacts, and changed permissions/restricted-user behavior in AP and AR.

## Breaking Changes & Deprecations

- Gantt charts in Sage Intacct Projects are being retired; resource planning reporting moves to PSA resource plans.
- Designated support users are now limited to 2 named contacts per company who may open cases with Support.
- Restricted-user capabilities have changed in both Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable — review what those users can still do.
- New permissions were added for Accounts Receivable and must be granted to affected roles.
- Smart event logging changed (no separate package install required) and the Audit Trail checkbox was removed from system-generated object fields.

## Action Required

- Identify and communicate your 2 designated support users before you need to open a case.
- Re-validate AP/AR role permissions and restricted-user access after upgrade.
- Projects teams using Gantt charts should plan a migration to PSA resource plan reporting.
- Contracts users: scheduled expense posting dates must now be on or after the expense template start date — existing templates may need correction.
- French entities should evaluate the new TVA Française tax solution and FEC export format for compliance reporting.

## New Capabilities (GA)

- Fixed Assets Management is now available, including automatic asset creation from purchasing transactions such as vendor invoices.
- Embedded Sage Intelligent Time is generally available, with a redesigned approvals experience, timesheet recall, and audit trail.
- PwC Control Insights for Sage Intacct is GA, providing prioritized internal-controls recommendations.
- Bank transaction assistant file import is GA with support for OFX and QIF files; Canadian bank file payments (CAD) are now supported.
- Blanket purchase and sales orders can now be converted by price rather than quantity, and employee expenses can be distributed via allocations.

## AI & Automation

- All AP Automation customers now receive AI predictions for GL account and selected dimensions.
- Sage Intelligent Time can automatically generate time entry descriptions via AI Assistant (beta).
