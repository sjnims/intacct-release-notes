# 2024 R1 Executive Summary

Sage Intacct 2024 R1 is a feature-expansion release with no breaking changes, headlined by three new integration/automation products — Sage Intacct Data Flow, Forms and Operational Workflows, and Professional Services Automation powered by Forecast. AI/ML capabilities expand through an Early Adopter program for line-level GL and dimension coding in AP Automation and expanded automated PO transaction matching. Construction customers get the most functional depth this cycle, with commitment compliance tracking, project contract billing details on AR invoices, and new reporting areas.
