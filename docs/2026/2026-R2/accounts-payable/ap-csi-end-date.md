# Process all Vendor payments powered by CSI by July 24, 2026

[Contact your Sage Intacct account manager](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Contact_your_account_manager)

Accounts Payable

## Action required

Process all payments by July 24, 2026 to avoid any potential issues.
