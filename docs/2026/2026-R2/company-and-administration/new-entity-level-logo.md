# New entity-level logo for AP bank remittance

[Entities](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Adding_Editing_and_Viewing_Entity_Information)

[Brand your company](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Brand_your_company)

[Company logo](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Upload_Company_Logo)

You can now upload entity‑level logos for AP bank remittance documents to clearly identify the issuing entity and meet regional compliance requirements where documents must reflect the correct entity.

## Key Benefits

- **Clear identification**: Logos help identify the entity (a subsidiary, business unit, or region) that issued a document.

- **Compliance support**: Comply with regional requirements in areas where invoices must clearly identify the issuing entity.

## How it works

Your entity-level logo will appear on bank remittance documents when the applicable template is used. If no entity-level logo is uploaded, your company logo will appear instead.

### Upload a logo at the entity level

1.  Go to **Company** > **Setup** > **Entities**.

2.  Find the entity that you want to upload a logo for.

3.  Select Edit at the end of the row.

4.  Select **Upload logo**.

5.  In the pop-up window, select **Choose Files**.

6.  Select a GIF, JPEG, or PNG image of your logo, then select **Open**.

7.  Select **Save**.

### File requirements

To ensure that your logo prints without distortion on forms, confirm that the logo is no larger than 0.3" wide x 0.6" high (216 pixels wide x 43 pixels high). The file can be saved either in GIF, JPEG, or PNG format, but GIF is preferable. File size cannot exceed 9K bytes.
