# SaaS Intelligence advanced analytics—Early Adopter

[SaaS Intelligence help center](https://bakertilly.clickhelp.co/articles/#!saas-intelligence-publication/getting-started)

[SaaS Intelligence announcement](https://bakertilly.clickhelp.co/articles/#!saas-intelligence-publication/getting-started)

INDUSTRY SOLUTIONS

We’re excited to share that SaaS Intelligence has received a major upgrade. SaaS Intelligence is now a next‑generation BI experience designed to meet the evolving insight needs of modern SaaS finance teams.

The new experience delivers more powerful, customizable, and flexible dashboards, giving you deeper visibility into revenue performance and key SaaS metrics. With expanded metrics, richer visuals, and predictive analytics, you can explore your data more easily and make more confident, forward‑looking decisions.

![An example dashboard](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R2-images/2026-R2-saas-intelligence.png)

## Key benefits

-   **Enhanced interactivity:** A modern, next‑generation interface with dynamic controls that make exploration faster and more intuitive.
    
-   **Increased ad‑hoc flexibility:** Quickly adjust metrics, timeframes, currencies, pivots, and more to get the exact view you need.
    
-   **Deeper segmentation:** Compare performance across product lines, industries, customer types, and other key segments with ease.
    
-   **Expanded metrics and visuals:** Access dozens of new ARR categories, churn cohort analyses, SaaS KPIs, and visualizations.
    
-   **Predictive insights:** ML‑powered forecasting and automated trend detection help you anticipate what’s coming next.
    

## Why the change

As your business grows and changes, your analytics need to keep pace. This upgrade moves SaaS Intelligence to a more robust BI foundation, unlocking greater scalability, speed, and flexibility for deeper, more dynamic analysis. It also lays the groundwork for continued innovation. You can rely on SaaS Intelligence as your source of clear, actionable insights.

## How it works

1.  Go to **SaaS Intelligence** > **Dashboards**.
    
    The page opens in a new browser tab.
    
2.  Select a dashboard.
    
3.  Use interactive controls to adjust metrics, timeframes, currencies, and segments to explore your data from multiple perspectives.
    

## What's the Early Adopter program?

The Early Adopter program is available to a select group of customers who test new features and provide feedback. Your input will help us refine and improve these features before the general release.

If you want to be considered for the Early Adopter program, reach out to [SaaSIntelligence@bakertilly.com](mailto:SaaSIntelligence@bakertilly.com).
