At Sage Intacct, we get how vital it is to have in-depth and best practice learning available to make sure you get the most out of Intacct.

- Access new and updated self-paced, on-demand courses that guide you through specific tasks and features.
- View demo videos to help you get up and running on a variety of tasks.

## New and updated training courses

You can find these new and updated training courses at [Sage University](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/).

You'll need to [log in to Sage University with your Sage account](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Connect_Sage_University).

| Application    | Course                                                 | Description                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| General Ledger | Improving Allocation Accuracy with Dynamic Allocations | This course teaches you how to use the Dynamic Allocations subscription in Sage Intacct to create accurate, consistent, and automated allocations based on live operational data. |

## New course format for Sage Intacct Fundamentals

The Sage Intacct Fundamentals training is now divided into 5 separate courses giving you more flexibility to complete the topics that apply best to your role.

If you're operating in the US or Canada, these new courses are:

- [Managing Journal Entries and the General Ledger (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mjeglna.html)

- [Managing Cash (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mcna.html)

- [Running Reports in Sage Intacct (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/rrsina.html)

- [Processing Accounts Receivable (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/par.html)

- [Processing Accounts Payable (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/pap.html)

If you're operating in the UK, Australia, or South Africa these new courses are:

- [Managing Journal Entries and the General Ledger (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mjegluk.html)

- [Managing Cash (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mcuk.html)

- [Running Reports in Sage Intacct (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/rrsina.html)

- [Processing Accounts Receivable (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/paruk.html)

- [Processing Accounts Payable (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/papuk.html)
