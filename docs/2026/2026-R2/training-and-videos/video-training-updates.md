At Sage Intacct, we get how vital it is to have in-depth and best practice learning that enhance your use of Intacct. To that end we recently:

-   Provided access to new and updated self-paced, on-demand courses that guide you through specific tasks and features.
-   Added new product demo videos to help you get up and running on a variety of tasks.

## New course format for Sage Intacct Fundamentals

The Sage Intacct Fundamentals training is now divided into 5 separate courses giving you more flexibility to complete the topics that apply best to your role.

If you're operating in the US or Canada, these new courses are:

-   [Managing Journal Entries and the General Ledger (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mjeglna.html)
    
-   [Managing Cash (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mcna.html)
    
-   [Running Reports in Sage Intacct (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/rrsina.html)
    
-   [Processing Accounts Receivable (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/par.html)
    
-   [Processing Accounts Payable (NA)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/pap.html)
    

If you're operating in the UK, Australia, or South Africa these new courses are:

-   [Managing Journal Entries and the General Ledger (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mjegluk.html)
    
-   [Managing Cash (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/mcuk.html)
    
-   [Running Reports in Sage Intacct (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/rrsina.html)
    
-   [Processing Accounts Receivable (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/paruk.html)
    
-   [Processing Accounts Payable (UK)](https://sageu.com/SageIntacct/direct/papuk.html)
    

## New product videos

These product videos are found in the [Video Library](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Intacct_basics/video-library.htm).

| Application | Video | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounts Payable | Void a payment | This video walks through how to void a payment in Accounts Payable. |
| Accounts Receivable | Receive a payment to undeposited funds | This video shows how to receive a payment to undeposited funds, and then deposit those undeposited funds. |
| Company and Administration | Sage Collaborate overview | This video walks you through using Sage Collaborate, a team communication tool that helps teams share information and stay updated on projects in real time. |
| General Ledger | Create an Account allocation definition | This video explores how to configure a dynamic account allocation to create a new Account allocation. |
| View the impact of Dynamic allocations | This video demonstrates how running a Dynamic allocation impacts the distribution of amounts in Sage Intacct. |
