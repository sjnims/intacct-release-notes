# Updated global navigation brings Sage products and tools together

We've updated the global navigation experience to help you move between Sage applications, access shared Sage tools, and quickly identify your product and company from a single, consistent location. The navigation supports both light and dark themes and is fully accessible using a keyboard.

Your existing Sage Intacct workflows, settings, and product navigation are unchanged. This update affects only the top-level header.

## Key benefits

- **Consistent experience across Sage products:** Work in a familiar navigation experience wherever you use Sage applications.

- **Faster access to applications:** Switch between Sage products from a single location.

- **Clear company and product context:** Quickly identify your product, company, and environment while you work.

- **Centralized access to Sage tools:** Access Copilot, Collaborate, Announcements, and Help and Support from one location.

## How it works

The navigation bar now includes the following components:

![" "](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R3-images/2026-R3-ui-global-nav.png)

1.  **App launcher:** Access other Sage products available to you.

2.  **Product and company information:** Quickly identify your product, company, and environment.

3.  **Copilot:** Get AI-powered assistance while you work.

4.  **Collaborate:** Connect with colleagues and shared workspaces.

5.  **Help and Support:** Access documentation, training, and support resources.

6.  **Announcements and Resource Center:** Stay up to date with product news and resources.

7.  **User menu:** Manage your account settings and preferences.

The navigation also displays your company logo if one has been configured.

The entity switcher, environment indicator, and other Intacct\-specific controls remain available.
