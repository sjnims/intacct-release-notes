# Vendor payments powered by CSI has been retired

[Contact your Sage Intacct account manager](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Contact_your_account_manager)

Accounts Payable

Vendor payments powered by CSI was retired with this release. To explore alternative solutions, contact your [account manager](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Contact_your_account_manager) or partner.

## Permissions and other requirements

| Subscription          | Vendor Payments powered by CSI      |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Regional availability | United States                       |
| User type             | Business user with admin privileges |
