# Announcing Sage Intacct daily sales report powered by Craftable

Craftable supports the unique workflows of hospitality businesses. Integrating Craftable and Sage Intacct connects your operational and financial data, giving you continuous visibility into daily sales.

[![Example of a daily sales report](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R3-images/2026-R3-craftable.png)](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R3-images/2026-R3-craftable.png)

## Key benefits

- Daily sales from every location and entity automatically post to General Ledger each night, so Finance starts each day with current numbers and keeps the close on schedule.
- Different locations can run different point of sale systems and still report through one consistent feed into Intacct.
- As your business grows, Sage Intacct's robust general ledger will continue to meet your needs, so you won't have to migrate your GL.
- Data about back-of-house flows to financials so you can always find complete and current information about your financials.
- Automated data flow and a more connected back-office experience reduces manual work and gives your teams faster access to the insights they need.
- Syncing of sales data from Craftable into Sage Intacct streamlines daily financial processes and improves visibility across locations.​
