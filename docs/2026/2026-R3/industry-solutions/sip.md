# Updates in Enhanced Sage Intacct Planning (eSIP)

BUDGETING AND PLANNING

We've recently made the following updates and improvements to eSIP.

- **Merge wizard updates**:

  - Preview changes prior to merge.
  - View impacted dependencies from the **Referenced by** column.
  - Flag and exclude changes that cause merge conflicts.
  - Select which changes to merge from one plan into another, or merge all changes.

  [Learn about using the merge wizard](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/merge-wizard.htm)

- **Add and delete plan lines**: You can add a new plan line (including statistical accounts plan lines) directly into your plan. You can also delete plan lines directly from your plan, from either income statement or statistical account grids. [Learn how to add or delete plan lines](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/add-delete-plan-line.htm).
- **Create a plan from an existing plan**: Use a plan as a template to quickly create a new plan for the upcoming planning cycle. The structure and plan lines remain the same. When you create a plan from plan, you can select whether to keep formulas as is or convert them to values. [Learn how to create a plan from plan](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/create-from-plan.htm).
- **Overwrite budgets**: You can overwrite a previously saved plan in the Sage Intacct Financials budget repository. [Learn how to save a plan as an Intacct budget](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/save-plan-as-Intacct-budget.htm).
- **Export as CSV**: Export your income statement accounts or statistical accounts to a CSV file. [Learn how to export to a CSV file](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/export-as-csv.htm).
- **Workforce planning**: Take control of employee-related expenses from the workforce tab. [Learn about workforce planning](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/TOC-workforce.htm).
- **Refresh actuals**: Refresh the actuals from Intacct Financials to keep your plan current and accurate. [Learn how to refresh actuals](https://help.intacct-planning.com/core/Topics/refresh-actuals.htm).
