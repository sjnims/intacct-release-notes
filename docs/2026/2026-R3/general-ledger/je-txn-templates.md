# Fast access to journal entry templates

[Memorized transactions for journal entries](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Adding_Editing_and_Viewing_a_Memorized_Transaction)

[Recurring journal entries](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Adding_a_Recurring_Journal_Entry)

[Transaction templates for journal entries](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Creating_Editing_and_Viewing_Transaction_Templates)

general ledger

You can access all types of journal entry templates, which can be used to memorize, schedule, and standardize entries, directly from the General Ledger > All menu.

![General Ledger All menu with Journal entry templates menus circled. ](https://preview.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R3-images/2026-R3-gl-txn-templates-submenu.png)

If your organization uses Global Consolidation, the transaction templates are available only at the entity level.

## Journal entries

Under Journal entries, the **Journal entry templates** sub-menu includes the following options:

- Memorized transaction templates

- Recurring transaction templates

- Transaction templates

## Statistical journal entries

Under Statistical journal entries, the **Journal entry templates** sub-menu includes the following options:

- Memorized transaction templates

- Recurring transaction templates
