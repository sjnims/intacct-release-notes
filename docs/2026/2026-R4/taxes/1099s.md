Prepare for 2026 tax year reporting by reviewing this year's form changes, verifying your 1099 setup, and deciding how you'll file forms.

## Prepare for filing season

Before you file:

1.  Review the Tax Year 2026 form changes and required actions below.

2.  Verify that your company, vendors, and employees are [set up for 1099 processing](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=TOC_1099_vendors).

3.  Decide whether you'll e-file or print and mail forms.

4.  Run the 1099 report and review reporting amounts.

## Filing options

[Option 1: E-file](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/2026/2026_Release_4/Taxes/2026-R4-1099s.htm#)

If you file 10 or more information returns, the IRS generally requires electronic filing. Use the TaxBandits integration to [e-file 1099 forms directly from Sage Intacct.](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Efile_1099_overview)

[Option 2: Print and mail forms](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/2026/2026_Release_4/Taxes/2026-R4-1099s.htm#)

If you plan to print forms, [order 1099 forms](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Order_1099_forms) from the supported forms provider. These forms are the **only** forms that we support. Purchasing forms from other vendors might result in printing issues.

After receiving the forms, print a test copy and verify the alignment before processing the complete set.

## 1099 form changes and required actions

Review the Tax Year 2026 form changes and any actions you need to take before filing.

Sage Intacct does not automatically move existing 1099 information when IRS form or box requirements change.

If you have transactions or initial values assigned to an affected form or box, review the information and move it to the appropriate new form or box as needed. See [Move 1099 information to another form or box](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Migrate_1099_boxes_and_forms).

[View detailed IRS form changes](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/2026/2026_Release_4/Taxes/2026-R4-1099s.htm#)

| Form                                | Form changes                                                                                                                                                                                         | What you need to do                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1099-MISC                           | The reporting threshold increases to $2,000. New boxes report on the following: Box 13a: Cash tips Box 13b: Treasury Tipped Occupation Code Box 14: Overtime compensation                            | No action is required solely because of the threshold change. Review your 1099 information and use the new boxes as applicable.        |
| 1099-NEC                            | The reporting threshold increases to $2,000. Former Box 1 is now 1a: Nonemployee compensation New boxes are: Box 1b: Cash tips Box 1c: Treasury Tipped Occupation Code Box 1d: Overtime compensation | Review information assigned to the former Box 1 and move it to the appropriate new box, if needed.                                     |
| 1099-R                              | Box 7 and its associated checkbox are now 7a and 7b. New boxes 7c and 7d are added. Box 8 and its associated percentage box are now 8a and 8b.                                                       | Review information assigned to the affected boxes and update it if needed.                                                             |
| 1099-G                              | New Box 10: Family leave benefits is added. Former Boxes 10a, 10b, and 11 are now 11a, 11b, and 12.                                                                                                  | Review existing transactions and initial values assigned to the renumbered boxes and move them to the appropriate new boxes if needed. |
| 1099-S                              | Box 2 is divided into Boxes 2a through 2c. Other boxes are added, removed, or rearranged for Tax Year 2026.                                                                                          | Review information assigned to Boxes 2, 5, and 6. Move existing information to the appropriate new box, if required.                   |
| 1099-INT, 1099-DIV, W-2G, 1099-PATR | No form changes for the 2026 tax year.                                                                                                                                                               | No action required.                                                                                                                    |
