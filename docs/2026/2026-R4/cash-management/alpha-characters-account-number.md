# Enter account number using letters and numbers

[Create a checking account](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Adding_Editing_and_Viewing_Checking_Accounts)

[Create a savings account](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Adding_Editing_and_Viewing_Savings_Accounts)

Cash Management

Include letters as well as numbers in the account number of a bank account for all account currencies. Previously, non-Euro currency accounts allowed only numbers.

## Key benefits

- **Comply with bank requirements**: Set up checking accounts and savings accounts as required by your bank without a limitation to only numeric characters in the **Account number** field.

- **Simplify bank account set up**: Experience less friction when creating bank accounts.
