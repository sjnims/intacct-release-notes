# Bank files to replace Standard ACH

[Transition to the bank files](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Transition_to_nacha_ach_file)

cash management

The Standard ACH payment method will be retired in a future release in 2027. To continue processing ACH payments after Standard ACH is retired, configure your company, vendors, and bank accounts to use bank file payments.

See [Transition from Standard ACH to bank files](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Transition_to_nacha_ach_file) for detailed instructions.
