# Post multiple draft transactions at once

Save time and reduce manual effort by posting transactions in bulk when you turn on enhanced lists. Previously, you could only post these transactions one at a time.

## Key benefits

- **Get more done with less effort**: Update multiple transactions at once instead of posting them one at a time.

- **Scale with ease**: Efficiently manage large volumes of transactions as your organization grows.

## How it works

On transaction list pages, we've added a checkbox column. When you select several draft transactions and then select **Post** at the top of the list, the selected transactions are posted without needing to open them individually.

![Order Entry transactions list showing selection checkboxes and Post button](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R4-images/2026-R4-oe-bulk-posts.png)
