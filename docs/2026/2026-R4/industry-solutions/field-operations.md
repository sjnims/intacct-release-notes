# Global search for Sage Field Operations

[About Sage Field Operations](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=sage-field-operations)

INDUSTRY SOLUTIONS

Find information more easily and quickly. You can now search through all data in Sage Field Operations from a single search bar, helping office and field service teams save time, speed up their workflows, and deliver better customer service.

## Key benefits

- Enter a search term once and get matching results from across Sage Field Operations; no more browsing separate lists to find a record.
- Search results are sorted to show the closest matches first, helping you find the right record with fewer clicks.

## Good to know

Global search covers the following record types:

- Locations
- Work orders
- Quotes
- Jobs
- Invoices
- Equipment
- Customer purchase orders
- WO and job assignments
