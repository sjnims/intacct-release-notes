# Announcing Sage Intacct Investment Intelligence—Early Adopter

Gain insight about your investments without leaving Sage Intacct. See portfolio performance, liquidity, and investment data in a single experience, helping your finance team make better-informed, more-confident investment decisions.

## Key benefits

- Connect investments and financials in one system.
- Turn portfolio activity into forward-looking insights.
- Centralize portfolio visibility and investment metrics.

We invite you to explore this feature as part of the Early Adopter program. Your feedback helps us improve the experience before broader release.
