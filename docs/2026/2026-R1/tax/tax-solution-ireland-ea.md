# Support for taxes in the Republic of Ireland—Early Adopter

[Set up standard tax solutions](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Set_up_taxes)

[Update a standard VAT or GST setup](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Update_standard_setup)

Taxes

To facilitate tax calculation and reporting, we added a tax solution for the Republic of Ireland to our list of standard tax solutions. This tax solution provides an automated setup for the tax rates for domestic VAT in the Republic of Ireland.

If your company or entity uses this tax solution, you can also install custom reports that give you summary- and detail-level insight into your taxable transactions.

![Screenshot of the Tax solution information page for Ireland VAT - SYS](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R1-images/2026-R1-RN-tax-solution-ireland-ea.png)

## Key benefits

- **Expanded regional support**: Companies that operate in the Republic of Ireland can use the Ireland tax solution to capture taxes on their transactions.

- **Tax reporting**: You can use custom reports to gain insight into your taxable transactions.

## What's the Early Adopter program?

The Early Adopter program is available to a select group of customers who test new features and provide feedback. Your input will help us refine and improve these features before the general release.
