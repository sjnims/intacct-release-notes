# Streamlined tax totals in converted transactions

[Convert an Order Entry transaction](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Converting_a_sales_transaction)

[Convert a Purchasing transaction](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=Converting_a_purchase_transaction)

Taxes

This idea came from you

Order Entry and Purchasing transactions with VAT and GST now display net and tax in a single line when you convert them to Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable transactions. This change provides a clearer view for transaction details and aligns the experience with direct AP/AR postings.

## Key benefits

- **Clearer transaction details**: No separate lines for net and tax, everything is in one place.

- **Consistent experience**: Matches the behavior of direct AP/AR postings and cash basis tax capture in France.

- **Global rollout**: Previously available only in France; now extended to all regions and transaction types.
