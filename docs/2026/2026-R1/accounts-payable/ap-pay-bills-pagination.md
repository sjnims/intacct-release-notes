# Pay bills enhancements—Early Adopter

[Pay bills](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/help_action/Default.htm#cshid=TOC_ap_workbench)

accounts payable

We're continuing our Early Adopter program for the enhanced Pay bills experience. And, starting this release, this experience is available automatically to new companies.

## Key benefits

Enjoy advanced filtering and usability improvements to help you process bills faster and more accurately, even at scale.

- **Faster bill processing**: Advanced filters and dynamic sorting help you find the bills you need quickly.

- **Handle more data**: Pay bills now supports high‑volume workloads without slowing down.

- **More accurate credit application**: Attribute‑based filters help ensure that credits apply to the right bills.

- **Simpler navigation**: A cleaner layout, persistent filters, and split vendor columns improve usability.

## What's changed

- Improved transaction handling: Load more bills at once—up to 5,000—while viewing 1,000 rows per page.

- Advanced filtering and sorting:

  - Filter for Vendor, Vendor ID, Bill number, Bill date, Due date, Currency, Amount due, Inline credit, and Preferred payment method.

  - Location-based and project-based credit filters with attribute-value pairs for precise credit application.

  - Persistent filters remain active when navigating between pages.

- Vendor column split: Vendor name and ID now appear in separate columns for easier search and sort.

- Preferred payment method search: Group payments by method (check, ACH, wire transfer) to streamline processing.

- Updated advanced filters: Intuitive design with improved table headings for Credit filters, Drill down filters, and Sort by.

- Sorting improvements: Dynamic sorting for key columns; note that some attributes (Payment priority, Discount date, Location ID/Name) are not supported.

![](https://www.intacct.com/ia/docs/en_US/releasenotes/Resources/Images/2026/2026-R1-images/2026-R1-ap-pay-bills.png)

## How it works

1.  Go to **Accounts Payable** > **Pay bills**.

2.  Use the **Apply filter** option to bring in up to 5,000 bills.

3.  Apply filters from the new filter panel: Select criteria such as Vendor range, Due date range, or Credit date range.

4.  Create an advanced filter to target location-based or project-based credits for precise credit application.

5.  Sort columns dynamically to organize bills by your preferred attributes.

6.  Review and process bills efficiently with the expanded grid and persistent filters.
