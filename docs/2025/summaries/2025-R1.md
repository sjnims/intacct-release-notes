# 2025 R1 Executive Summary

Sage Intacct 2025 R1 delivers two headline items with hard deadlines: you must authenticate and validate your custom email domain before May 2025, and the Authorize.net and PayPal customer payment integrations retire on November 7, 2025. On the capability side, AP Automation with Purchasing and the REST API both reach general availability, and Copilot expands with a new Close Assistant early adopter program and GA Variance Analysis (UK/US). Most finance teams should focus this quarter on the two migration deadlines while evaluating AP/Purchasing automation and the new configurable Lists experience.

## Breaking Changes & Deprecations

- Authorize.net and PayPal customer payment integrations will be discontinued on November 7, 2025; Sage recommends migrating to Versapay.
- Custom email domains must be authenticated and validated before May 2025 or email deliverability from Intacct will be at risk (all regions).
- WIP Management database field names have been renamed, which may break existing reports or integrations (AU, CA, US).
- Permission menus changed this release and should be reviewed against existing user roles.
- UI labels were updated across AP, AR, Cash Management, Company, Fixed Assets, Inventory, Order Entry, Projects, Purchasing, and WIP — check documentation and training materials.

## Action Required

- Complete custom email domain authentication ahead of the May 2025 deadline.
- Plan and budget a customer payment gateway migration before the November 7, 2025 cutoff.
- Canadian companies must install the updated standard tax solution to pick up Nova Scotia's April 2025 rate decrease.
- Audit permission and vendor/customer visibility changes before users encounter them in production.

## New Capabilities (GA)

- AP Automation with Purchasing is now available to all companies, auto-matching incoming vendor invoices to purchase orders and receivers (all regions).
- NACHA-compliant ACH payment file generation is now available (US); new Canadian bank file payment formats and French-language cheque printing added for Canada.
- Fixed asset transfers between dimensions (departments, projects) now generate GL entries automatically and log to a Transfer History tab (all regions).
- Revenue Management can now process, defer, and recognize revenue from third-party billing applications (AU, CA, ZA, UK, US).
- Vendor-based bill approval rules and expanded purchase order approval delegation, including restricted users as delegates and user groups as approvers (all regions).
- General Ledger can now block direct posts on a journal-by-journal basis to reduce subledger-to-trial-balance discrepancies (all regions).
