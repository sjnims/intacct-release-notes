# 2025 R2 Executive Summary

**BLUF:** 2025 R2 is a heavy platform and automation release: the affiliate entity dimension, the new AI-powered import service, Spanish/Canadian French language support, and REST API Bulk Request Service all reach general availability, while Copilot's Subledger Reconciliation Assistant and mobile expense automation enter Early Adopter. The most time-sensitive items are the November 7, 2025 retirement of Authorize.net and PayPal customer payment integrations (US) and the May 2025 deadline to authenticate your custom email domain. Fixed Assets Management, Construction, and Contracts also received substantial functional upgrades.

## Breaking Changes & Deprecations

- Authorize.net and PayPal customer payment integrations retire November 7, 2025; Versapay is the recommended replacement (US).
- The classic import experience for bank/credit card reconciliation is retiring in favor of the Bank Transaction Assistant file import.
- All new API objects and features will ship only in the REST API — the XML API is supported but frozen for new functionality.
- Special characters are now restricted in certain AP and AR fields, and importing non-draft invoices/adjustments now requires Post permissions.
- Time and Expenses are now split into two separate applications with dedicated menus (subscription unchanged); AR "Receive a payment" retirement has been postponed.

## Action Required

- Authenticate and validate your custom email domain during May 2025 to avoid email delivery failures to customers and partners.
- US customers taking card/online payments must plan a migration off Authorize.net/PayPal before November 7, 2025.
- Review changed permissions across Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Expenses, Projects, and Time, plus new Post permission requirements on imports.
- Move any reconciliation import processes to the Bank Transaction Assistant before the classic importer is removed.

- Affiliate entity standard dimension for automatic inter-entity tagging and more granular consolidation eliminations (all regions, Consolidation subscribers).

- Bidirectional payment sync between Salesforce and Intacct, completing the quote-to-cash workflow.
- Sage Intacct Fundraising powered by DonorPerfect (Canada, US) and the Tessitura Ticketing Connector in Data Flow (US) for nonprofits.
